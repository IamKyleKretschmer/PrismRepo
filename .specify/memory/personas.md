# Customer Personas

Reference personas for feature spec decisions. All personas use Workflow, Process Manager, Insights, and Apps.

## Carmine — App & Asset Creator

- **Role:** Ops manager, low-code builder, bridges business ↔ IT
- **Skills:** Technical 3 · Business 4 · Collaboration 4 · Problem Solving 5 · Data 4
- **Needs:** Build simple solutions without IT dependency, align automation with business goals, translate business needs into technical requirements
- **Pain points:** Documentation gaps → inefficiency, undefined success criteria → delays, overwhelmed by request volume, limited resources for complex projects

## Eric — Engineer

- **Role:** IT developer, builds custom integrations (CSS/JS), supports internal teams on NAC/RPA
- **Skills:** Technical 5 · Business 3 · Collaboration 3 · Problem Solving 5 · Data 4
- **Needs:** Solve complex integration challenges, digitise paper-based processes, stay current with platform capabilities
- **Pain points:** Delayed roadmap features blocking planned solutions, opaque workflow errors and undiagnosable error messages

## Lara — Line of Business (LOB)

- **Role:** Inventory manager, consumes apps built by IT, manages team operations via dashboards and views
- **Skills:** Technical 2 · Business 5 · Collaboration 4 · Problem Solving 3 · Data 4
- **Needs:** Onboard team members easily, monitor order progress and team efficiency, generate reports for decision-making
- **Pain points:** Cannot diagnose/restart failed workflows, hard to locate completed forms, team struggles to access platform assets (process maps, checklists, forms, tables)

## Ursula — Participant / End User

- **Role:** Incident case manager, follows prescribed forms and processes, needs oversight across many cases
- **Skills:** Technical 2 · Business 4 · Collaboration 4 · Problem Solving 3 · Data 5
- **Needs:** Clear case oversight, performant system with complete/accurate/validated data, swift incident resolution
- **Pain points:** Data scattered across systems (resorts to spreadsheets), context-switching between products, notification overload, chasing colleagues for reports/data
